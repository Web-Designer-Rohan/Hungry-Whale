# Team & Process Question Bank

## Ownership & Accountability

1. Who owns each service or component? Single owner? Bus factor?
2. What is the ownership model? Service ownership? Feature teams? Guilds?
3. What is the accountability for incidents? On-call? Blameless postmortems?
4. Who decides what? Architecture? Priorities? Process? Tooling?
5. What is the escalation path for unresolved issues? Blockers? Conflicts?

## Team Structure

6. What is the team composition? Roles? Skills mix? Seniority balance?
7. What is the team size? Optimal? Growth plan?
8. What is the team topology? Cross-functional? Squads? Chapters?
9. What are the interfaces between teams? APIs? Contracts? Dependencies?
10. What is the team autonomy? Deploy independently? Choose tools? Own backlog?

## Communication

11. What are the communication channels? Slack? Email? Meetings? Async?
12. What is the meeting load? Cadence? Agendas? Outcomes? Action items?
13. What is the async-first policy? Written decisions? Documentation?
14. How are decisions communicated? ADRs? RFCs? Announcements? Recording?
15. What is the stakeholder communication? Cadence? Format? Audience?

## Onboarding & Knowledge

16. What is the onboarding process? Checklist? Buddy? Timeline? First-week goals?
17. What is the documentation quality? Fresh? Searchable? Owned?
18. What is the bus factor per area? Knowledge silos? Cross-training?
19. What is the knowledge sharing cadence? Tech talks? Brown bags? Demos?
20. What is the mentoring program? Senior-to-junior? Formal or informal?

## Decision Making

21. What is the decision-making framework? Consensus? RACI? DACI?
22. Who has authority for product decisions? Tech decisions? Process decisions?
23. What is the RFC/ADR process? Template? Review? Timeline?
24. What is the dispute resolution? Escalation? Tie-breakers? Who arbitrates?
25. Which decisions are reversible vs irreversible? How is that assessed?

## Process & Rituals

26. What is the agile process? Scrum? Kanban? Cadence? Ceremonies?
27. What is the planning process? Roadmap? Sprint planning? Estimation method?
28. What is the retro process? Frequency? Action items? Follow-through?
29. What is the standup format? Async? Written? Live? Time-boxed?
30. What is the definition of done? Merged? Shipped? Monitored? Documented?
31. What is the backlog hygiene? Triage? Prioritization? Staleness? WIP limits?

## Hiring & Growth

32. What is the hiring plan? Roles? Timeline? Interview process? Pipeline?
33. What is the career progression? Ladder? Criteria? Promotion process?
34. What is the skills gap analysis? Training budget? Upskilling?
35. What is the performance review process? Cadence? 360? Calibration?
36. What is the retention strategy? Engagement surveys? Exit interviews? Action?

## Culture & Morale

37. What is the team culture? Values? Behaviors? Rituals?
38. What is the psychological safety? Blame-free? Speak-up? Experimentation?
39. What is the work-life balance? On-call load? Overtime? Burnout signals?
40. What is the recognition program? Peer? Manager? Public?
41. What is the diversity and inclusion posture? Practices? Metrics? Accountability?
42. What is the remote/hybrid policy? Async? Overlap hours? Travel cadence?

## Cross-Team & External

43. What are the dependencies on other teams? How are they managed? Blockers?
44. What is the vendor/partner management? SLAs? Review cadence? Escalation?
45. What is the open source policy? Contributing? Publishing? Licensing?
46. What is the compliance training? Cadence? Coverage? Tracking?

## Process Improvement

47. What is the improvement loop? Metrics? Experiments? Review cadence?
48. What is the toil reduction plan? Automation targets? Measurement?
49. What is the innovation time? Hack days? 20% time? Experiment budget?
50. What is the team's one-year vision? Success criteria? How is it measured?
