# Product Question Bank

## Problem & User

### Problem Definition
1. What is the problem in one sentence? (Not the solution — the problem)
2. Who has this problem? Be specific: role, context, frequency, severity.
3. How do they solve it today? Workarounds? Competitors? Manual process?
4. What is the cost of the problem? Time? Money? Frustration? Risk?
5. Why now? What changed? Why not 6 months ago? Why not 6 months from now?

### User Understanding
6. What are the top 3 user personas? For each: goals, pain points, technical skill, context.
7. What is the user's mental model of the problem space?
8. What are the user's current tools? What do they love/hate about them?
9. What would make the user switch? What would make them stay?
10. How do you access users for research? How often?

## Value Proposition

### Core Value
11. What is the unique value proposition? Complete this: "Only we provide [X] for [Y] who need [Z]."
12. What is the "aha!" moment? When does the user first realize value?
13. What is the minimum viable value? What can you cut and still deliver value?
14. What are the moats? Network effects? Data? Brand? Switching costs? IP?
15. What is the pricing model? Why? What are the alternatives?

### Differentiation
16. Who are the direct competitors? Indirect? Substitutes?
17. What do competitors do better? What do you do better?
18. What is your wedge? Beachhead market? Expansion path?
19. What is the build vs. buy vs. partner decision for each component?
20. What is the exit strategy? (Acquisition, IPO, profitability, shutdown)

## Metrics & Success

### North Star
21. What is the ONE metric that matters? (Retention? Revenue? Usage? NPS?)
22. What are the leading indicators? Lagging indicators?
23. What is the target for Month 1? 3? 6? 12?
24. What is the counter-metric? (What you must NOT degrade)
25. How do you measure "delight" vs. "utility"?

### Activation & Retention
26. What is the activation event? Time to value?
27. What is the retention curve? Day 1? Day 7? Day 30? Day 90?
28. What are the churn reasons? Voluntary? Involuntary?
29. What is the expansion revenue path? Upsell? Cross-sell? Usage-based?
30. What is the LTV:CAC ratio? Payback period?

## Prioritization & Roadmap

### Framework
31. What prioritization framework? RICE? ICE? WSJF? Custom?
32. What are the top 5 opportunities? Score them.
33. What is the cost of delay for each?
34. What are the dependencies between opportunities?
35. What is the capacity allocation? (New features vs. tech debt vs. bugs vs. experiments)

### Scope
36. What is in scope for MVP? What is explicitly OUT?
37. What is the "walk away" criteria? When do you kill it?
38. What are the regulatory/compliance constraints?
39. What are the legal/IP risks?
40. What are the brand/reputation risks?

## Go-to-Market

### Launch
41. What is the launch strategy? Beta? Gradual rollout? Big bang?
42. Who are the first 10 customers? 100? 1000?
43. What is the launch checklist? (Legal, security, support, docs, marketing)
44. What is the rollback plan?
45. What is the post-launch monitoring plan?

### Growth
46. What are the acquisition channels? Cost? Scale? Time to results?
47. What is the viral coefficient? Referral program?
48. What is the content/SEO strategy?
49. What is the partnership strategy?
50. What is the internationalization plan?

## Product Operations

### Feedback Loops
51. How do you collect feedback? Quantitative? Qualitative? Continuous?
52. What is the experiment velocity? A/B tests per month?
53. What is the feature adoption tracking? Sunset process?
54. What is the support volume? Top issues? Time to resolution?
55. What is the product analytics stack? Events? Properties? Identity?

### Team & Process
56. What is the product team structure? PM:Eng ratio? Design:Eng?
57. What is the sprint cadence? Planning process? Retro process?
58. What is the stakeholder management? Review cadence?
59. What is the decision-making authority? PM? Tech lead? Committee?
60. What is the hiring plan? Skills gaps?
